💡 Connection Message Tips (for higher acceptance)
  • Mention something specific: "I saw your post on AI in education" or "I'm also a BYU-Idaho software student"
  • Keep it under 3 sentences — LinkedIn limits notes to 300 characters anyway
  • Share a quick credential: "I'm building CurriculumCraft AI for Ghanaian teachers using Next.js + GitHub Models"
  • Don't ask for a job in the connection request — build the relationship first
  • Follow up with a comment on their post within 48 hrs of connecting to stay visible
  • Target 2nd-degree connections first — you share a mutual contact, so acceptance rates are 3–4× higher
